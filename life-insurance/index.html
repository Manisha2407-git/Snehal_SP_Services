Life Insurance

Life insurance ensures your family remains financially protected in case of any unforeseen event. It creates a long-term safety net and supports goals such as children’s education, retirement planning, and family security.

Types of Life Insurance

  • Term Life Insurance — High coverage at low premiums.
  • Money-Back & Endowment Plans — Savings + insurance benefit.
  • Retirement & Pension Plans — Monthly income after retirement.
  • Child Plans — Secure education & future expenses.

Benefits

  • Financial protection for family
  • Tax benefits under 80C
  • Low premium for high sum assured
  • Lump-sum payout in emergencies

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the minimum premium??

Term plans start at very affordable premiums depending on age and coverage.

2. Can I add riders?

Yes. Add accidental, critical illness, waiver of premium, and more.

3. Is medical test required?

Only for high coverage or age above specified limits.
