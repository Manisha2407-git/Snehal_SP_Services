Health Insurance

Health insurance protects you and your family against expensive medical treatments, surgeries, and emergencies. It ensures the best healthcare without financial burden.

Coverage Includes

  • Hospitalisation expenses
  • Day-care procedures
  • Cashless treatment at network hospitals
  • Critical illness cover
  • Emergency ambulance

Why Choose Health Insurance?

  • Protects savings from medical expenses
  • Offers peace of mind during emergencies
  • Tax benefits under Section 80D

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the waiting period for health insurance?

Typically 30 days for new policies. Pre-existing diseases have 2–4 years waiting period.

2. Can I add parents to my policy?

Yes. Family floater plans allow parents, spouse & children.

3. Is cashless treatment available?

Yes. Available in network hospitals across India.
