Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why should I buy insurance through Snehal SP Services?

We provide unbiased recommendations, assist with paperwork, offer multiple insurers, and guide you end-to-end until policy issuance.

2. Which documents are required for insurance?

Basic KYC (Aadhaar, PAN), recent photo, income details (for some plans), and medical reports (for health/life plans above certain coverage).

3. How long does it take to get the policy?

Life insurance takes 2–5 working days. Health and general insurance can be issued instantly if documents are complete.

4. Do you provide claim assistance?

Yes, we guide you in the complete claim process and coordinate with the insurer for faster resolution.

5. Are there any extra service charges?

No. We do not charge customers anything. Insurance premium is paid directly to the company.
